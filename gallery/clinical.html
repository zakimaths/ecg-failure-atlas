Export and replay

Download the selected lead’s samples, settings, coefficients, measurements and source attribution. Replay checks against the bundled PTB samples, then independently repeats the calculation in Python.

Local replay command
uv sync --locked
uv run --locked ecg-atlas replay-clinical analysis.json

The replay checks the selected record and lead, source metadata, all samples, filter coefficients and measurements with 1e-10 absolute/relative numerical tolerance. Recorded data are loaded from the pinned subset; they cannot be regenerated from a mathematical model.

DATA PROVENANCE

PTB Diagnostic ECG Database v1.0.0

Contains information from the PTB Diagnostic ECG Database, made available under the ODC Attribution License v1.0. Collected by Michael Oeff at University Clinic Benjamin Franklin, Berlin; provided by PTB through PhysioNet.

Selection: the first listed record from each of the first three subject directories, using the first eight seconds of the 12 standard leads. This convenience subset is not a representative cohort. Original digital counts are retained without resampling, normalisation or denoising. Clinical header comments are omitted.
